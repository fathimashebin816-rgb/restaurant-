## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful nutrition, culinary vitality, and organic simplicity. Tailored for health-conscious urban professionals, wellness enthusiasts, and balanced-diet diners, it delivers an interface that feels invigorating, wholesome, and effortless.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Organic Warmth** with **Clean Editorial Functionalism**:
- Natural softness achieved through warm dairy-cream canvas backgrounds rather than cold digital whites.
- Vibrant plant-derived greens balance an energetic citrus accent to drive appetite and engagement.
- Tactile, rounded structural elements and pill badges echo organic forms without sacrificing transaction-oriented clarity.
- Crisp whitespace and rhythmic micro-interactions instill confidence in food quality, ingredient transparency, and delivery timeliness.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in earth and harvest notes, contrasting crisp botanical tones against soft, appetizing warmth.

- **Primary (`#2E7D32`)**: Fresh chlorophyll green conveying organic integrity, nutritional balance, and health. Used for core brand elements, primary headers, active selection outlines, and verified nutritional highlights.
- **Secondary (`#FF7A30`)**: Zesty carrot-citrus orange. Acts as the functional call-to-action engine: add-to-cart triggers, conversion CTAs, price highlights, and energetic promotion tags.
- **Tertiary (`#758A7A`)**: Muted sage gray-green. Deployed for secondary iconography, nutritional category tags, micro-copy, and subtle active tab indicators.
- **Neutral (`#212529`)**: Deep garden charcoal providing optimal readability and contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Background Tones**: Canvas utilizes `#FCF9F2` (soft warm cream) with inner elevated cards resting on `#FFFFFF` and structured with delicate border framing via `#E8E3D7`.
- **Status Semantic Palette**:
  - *New*: `#0284C7` (Sky blue background `#E0F2FE`)
  - *Preparing*: `#EA580C` (Warm amber background `#FFEDD5`)
  - *Ready*: `#16A34A` (Fresh green background `#DCFCE7`)
  - *Delivered*: `#475569` (Slate soft background `#F1F5F9`)
  - *Cancelled*: `#DC2626` (Muted red background `#FEE2E2`)

## Typography

The type system is powered exclusively by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering geometric clarity, contemporary warmth, and friendly rounded terminals that pair naturally with healthy, organic dining.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Display high clarity with tighter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to retain punchiness in food item naming and nutritional hero values.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for legibility across variable dietary listings, macro counts, allergen disclaimers, and culinary notes. Set primarily in dark charcoal (`#212529`) and muted charcoal (`#495057`).
- **Labels & Micro-data**: Deployed in medium and semi-bold weights for badges, ingredient pills, nutritional metrics (calories, carbs, protein), and cart stepper indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined, fluid 8pt/4pt layout grid brings visual order to dense ordering workflows:

- **Mobile Viewports (< 640px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Vertical rhythmic stack prioritizes menu item imagery, immediate macro highlights, and a sticky floating tray checkout button.
- **Tablet Viewports (640px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Food catalogs shift into an adaptive 2-column or 3-column card matrix.
- **Desktop Viewports (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout max-width constrained to `1280px` with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates side-by-side split layouts for meal customization, nutritional breakdowns, and live order tracking.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines warm organic layering with ambient, tinted drop shadows to create a clean, tactile tabletop feel:

- **Base Layer (Level 0)**: Warm canvas `#FCF9F2` devoid of shadow.
- **Surface Layer (Level 1 - Cards, Menu Rows)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with a soft warm border `1px solid #E8E3D7` and an ambient glow shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(33, 37, 41, 0.03)`.
- **Raised Interactive Layer (Level 2 - Steppers, Hovered Food Cards)**: Border shifts to `#D6CEC0` with elevated warmth: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(33, 37, 41, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays (Level 3 - Cart Drawer, Modals, Sticky Summary Bars)**: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(33, 37, 41, 0.12), 0 6px 12px -3px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.04)` over a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with `rgba(252, 249, 242, 0.8)` for modal underlays.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts smooth, welcoming curves that reinforce organic produce and culinary craftsmanship:

- **Standard Cards & Meal Modules**: Styled with generous `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) curvature.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Form Inputs)**: `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) to provide balanced ergonomics and approachable touch targets.
- **Pills & Badges**: Fully circular / pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for nutritional callouts, order statuses, and category filter chips.
- **Quantity Steppers**: Rounded encapsulated pill pods (`rounded-full`) maintaining tactile thumb interaction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Checkout, Add to Cart)**: Solid `#FF7A30` vibrant orange background, `#FFFFFF` bold label text, `rounded-xl`, padded with `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle hover translates `-1px` vertically with shadow intensification (`0 6px 16px rgba(255, 122, 48, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action (View Nutrition, Dietary Details)**: Warm white `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5px solid #2E7D32`, text `#2E7D32`. Hover switches background to a tinted organic wash `rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost (Clear Filters, Back)**: Transparent background, text `#495057`, hover `#F0EBE1`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Category Filter (e.g., Keto, Vegan, High-Protein)**: Height `36px`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0 1rem`. Unselected state utilizes white surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E3D7`, and text `#495057`. Selected state adopts `#2E7D32` surface with `#FFFFFF` text and no border.

### Status Badges
- High-contrast, all-caps or title-case pill badges (`rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, `label-sm` typography):
  - **New**: Text `#0369A1`, Background `#E0F2FE`, Border `1px solid #BAE6FD`.
  - **Preparing**: Text `#C2410C`, Background `#FFEDD5`, Border `1px solid #FED7AA`.
  - **Ready**: Text `#15803D`, Background `#DCFCE7`, Border `1px solid #BBF7D0`.
  - **Delivered**: Text `#334155`, Background `#F1F5F9`, Border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
  - **Cancelled**: Text `#B91C1C`, Background `#FEE2E2`, Border `1px solid #FECACA`.

### Cards (Food & Meal Items)
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, rounded `rounded-2xl`, border `1px solid #E8E3D7`.
- Generous internal padding (`1.25rem`). Top photography zone with soft inset radius (`rounded-xl`).
- Bottom section houses title, concise macro row (Calories / Protein / Carbs / Fat) in muted sage `#758A7A`, price in `#212529` font-bold, and compact Add button.

### Quantity Stepper
- Compact pill container (`rounded-full`, border `1px solid #E8E3D7`, background `#FCF9F2`).
- Integrated circular `-` and `+` hit targets with active feedback, centering a bold tabular numeric count (`title-sm`, `#212529`).

### Input Fields & Search
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E3D7`, corner radius `rounded-xl`, height `48px`, horizontal padding `1rem`.
- Focus state invokes a crisp `2px solid #2E7D32` ring with soft outer halo `rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.15)`. Placeholder colored in muted sage `#758A7A`.

### Nutrition Macro Matrix
- Modular micro-metric cards embedded into meal detail views. Soft cream background (`#F7F3EA`), rounded `rounded-xl`, padding `0.75rem`, showing clean vertical stacks: numerical value (`title-md`, `#212529`) over macro type (`label-sm`, `#758A7A`).
